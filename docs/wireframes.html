Bilge pump cycling every ~4 min

high Open ▾ Edit
targetBilge pump (aft) · Rule 2000
first noticed2026-10-18
symptomsPump runs ~5 s every 4 min at the dock, dry bilge. Started after heavy rain.
suspected causeSiphon back through discharge hose, or check valve failed
est. cost$60–200
Linked service entries
  • 9/14Replaced float switch (did not resolve)
Log service that resolves this
Notes / diagnosis thread
10/18youNoticed after rain. Bilge looks dry when it runs.
10/20youChecked float switch, moves freely. Pump itself seems fine.
10/28claudeService log shows the float switch was replaced 9/14 so that's unlikely. Rule 2000 is on a hose run to the transom thru-hull with no vented loop listed in Systems. A dry bilge with regular cycling points to water siphoning back down the discharge hose and re-triggering the switch. Suggest: check for a check valve or vented loop in the discharge line, and confirm the thru-hull sits above the waterline at rest. Est. $40 for a vented loop.
